Self-Assessment Quiz: Web Architecture & Protocols

Static content delivery and client-side interactivity stand as foundational capabilities of modern web development, each providing the framework and limits of user engagement. While reading informational pages establishes theoretical familiarity with networking protocols, active self-assessment reinforces how each concept reflects the technical needs it was designed to meet. By contrast with static reading, this dynamic frontend quiz allows you to test your comprehension of Uniform Resource Locators (URLs), the Hypertext Transfer Protocol (HTTP), Domain Name System (DNS) addressing, and client-server request cycles.

You are allotted 10 minutes to complete all ten questions. Generally speaking, achieving a threshold of 70% (7 out of 10 points) is required to pass.

Time Limit: 10 Minutes Time Remaining: 10:00

Assessment Results & Diagnostic Breakdown

Question 1 (Fill in the Blank — 1 Point)

Because numeric IPv4 addresses are not very memorable for quotidian browsing, the Internet relies on the ___ to translate human-friendly domain names (such as www.spsu.edu) into numeric IP addresses. (Enter the 3-letter acronym or full name.)

Question 2 (Fill in the Blank — 1 Point)

Within a URL, the part placed after the # symbol—also known as an anchor or _______ identifier—acts as a bookmark inside the document and is never sent to the web server with the HTTP request.

Question 3 (Multiple Choice — 1 Point)

Question 4 (Multiple Choice — 1 Point)

Question 5 (Multiple Choice — 1 Point)

Question 6 (Multiple Choice — 1 Point)

Question 7 (Multiple Choice — 1 Point)

Question 8 (Multiple Choice — 1 Point)
